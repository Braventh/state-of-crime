# Crownwater gameplay systems

## District

Crownwater is a 3,480-unit east–west region: Lantern Row, Rainline Market, Needle Alley and Glasshouse Works. Three clusters of fire escapes and multiple roof routes provide alternate paths above the street. The highest accessible surfaces sit at about y=190 relative to street y=414 in a 540-unit frame (42% of the frame); the road has separate vehicle lanes below it. Bluehour Arcade, Rainline Service Tunnel and Glasshouse Lockup each connect two doors through covered passages. Roofs have longer sightlines, weapon pickups and jumps across gaps. Covered routes block police sight and outdoor fire.

## Encounters, weapons and money

Pedestrians flee after violence; merchants hold storefronts or panic. Vesper Toll, Canal Thread and Glasshouse Union have distinct palettes, territories, pace, attack frequency and equipment. Attacking one member or stealing a crew vehicle can trigger a group response. Crews defend a limited territory and can fight nearby police. Downed crew members drop cash and gear. Robberies pay immediately, consume the target, and can be reported by witnesses.

Fists and pipe use short directional attacks with stagger. The pistol is the inexpensive all-rounder, shotgun gains damage up close, SMG fires rapidly, and rifle trades reload time for range and precision. Aiming sets the shot angle; floors block attacks and indoor and outdoor spaces are separate. Reload draws from reserve, duplicate firearm pickups add ammo, and switching interrupts a reload. Shops sell weapons, refills, health, clothing, hair, paints and vehicle upgrades.

## Vehicles and police

Parked and slow occupied cars can be stolen. Compact, coupe and van have distinct acceleration, grip, top speed and integrity. Shift one lane at a time, brake, and slow down to exit. Wrecks and impacts can add heat.

| Wanted | Response |
| --- | --- |
| ★ | Beat officers approach or follow on foot |
| ★★ | A cruiser can join the chase, with a ram warning |
| ★★★ | Armed officers and intercept dispatch |
| ★★★★ | Increased presence, more aggressive interception and faster rams |
| ★★★★★ | Maximum deployment and a passable center-lane roadblock |

Crimes add severity-weighted points, increased by witnesses, direct police observation or repeat offending. Gunfire and serious collisions can be reported as noise. Police remember the last sighting; delayed reports indicate an approximate location. Ground units do not track through passages or solid roof geometry. Dispatch spawns off-camera only if a sufficiently distant valid street position exists. Break sight, stop offending and move away or use cover for the search to decay. High stars take longer to lose.

Clothing and hair are independent recognition signals. In a car, vehicle identity and paint replace the on-foot appearance signal. Buying a change under active observation is denied; a successful change lowers reacquisition range rather than deleting evidence or heat. Police can still recognize you at close range, especially at high stars.
