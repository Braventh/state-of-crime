# Geographical masterplan

See [the relationship map](WORLD_MAP.md). The played coast borders a shallow bay. Crownwater sits where the mainland road reaches the inlet; a string of low keys supports the causeway to the outer shipping shore. Breakwater Yard has protected water and a highway feed for heavy freight. Reedwake drains inland water toward the inlet; Sawtooth Junction lies on the higher inland highway where agriculture and goods traffic meet.

The three implemented districts are continuous. Reedwake and Sawtooth Junction are planned branches, not selectable stages. Limited crossings, water clearance and freight access are the anchors for their future routes. No real or proprietary map underlies these positions.
