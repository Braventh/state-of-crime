# Mission design

| Job | Contact | Win condition | Failure / recovery | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First Light | Mara, Dispatch | Borrow and drive a car; bring the reel through Tidelane | Incapacitation or disabled ride; restart at latest checkpoint or contact | $300 |
| Tail Lights | Mara, Tidelane | Take the loaner Sable, lose active pursuit and check in at Bluehour | 58 s, disabled ride or incapacitation | $420 |
| Dead Air | Mara, Bluehour | On foot, take the Signal Exchange cartridge; evade or hide, then return | Disabled ride or incapacitation | $570 |
| Cut Across | Roux, causeway | Beat Roux in a rolling lane race to the east toll | Rival reaches finish, timeout, disabled ride or incapacitation | $630 |
| Last Transmission | Inez, Freight Office | Clear three sentries, platform onto the orange beacon crate, return to Inez | Disabled ride or incapacitation; sentries reset on retry | $850 |

A job's dialogue appears on acceptance; the objective panel and marker persist. The contact becomes available again after an ordinary failure. A cleared screen toast, immediate payout and fresh waypoint confirm completion. Shops and refuges exist physically on the strip rather than in a route picker.
