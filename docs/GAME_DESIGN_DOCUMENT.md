# Game design: The Palmetto Tapes

In Palmetto State, independent radio runners Mara and Inez are trying to get the only copies of a lost broadcast out of a freight consortium's hands. The player begins as Mara's courier. Roux runs the island toll route. The fifth job closes the broadcast arc without closing the sandbox.

The loop is **contact → read the objective → move or drive → break pursuit or complete the task → reinvest**. All three districts are accessible from the start, with mission contacts appearing in sequence. Borrowed rides raise heat; garage rides are safe to enter and can carry tune and armor upgrades. A minimap-style strip and distance cues support navigation at speed.

The vertical slice favors one continuous dusk route, three vehicle profiles, four levels of heat and five distinct jobs over a broad simulation. See the [masterplan](GEOGRAPHICAL_MASTERPLAN.md), [mission design](MISSION_DESIGN.md) and [release notes](RELEASE_NOTES.md).
