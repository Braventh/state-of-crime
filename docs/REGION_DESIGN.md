# Region design

| Region | Status | Identity | Traversal & landmarks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crownwater | Playable | Rose neon, apartments, ribbed towers, palms at the sea wall | Tidelane, Sidecar Garage, Signal Exchange, roof platforms |
| Sunsplice Keys | Playable | Coral resorts, pastel roofs, docks and open-water spans | Ferry light, refuge and timed causeway race |
| Breakwater Yard | Playable | Amber gantries, warehouse sawtooths, stacks of freight | Cargo contact, platformed containers, coded beacon |
| Reedwake Basin | Planned | Mangroves, dark tannin water, boardwalks and sparse lamps | Restricted dirt access branching from Crownwater; water forms barriers |
| Sawtooth Junction | Planned | Flat farms, canopies, diners and roadside motels | Inland highway branch past the bay; truck stops connect to the port |
