# World research

The only borrowed ideas are broad environmental contrasts. No reference art, maps, locations, audio or proprietary assets are used in-game.

| Reference | Public observation (high confidence) | Interpretation (medium confidence) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Official Rockstar GTA VI site and location galleries](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) | Official material names Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga, and presents a dense tropical city, islands, wetlands, working industrial settlements and backcountry. | Concentrate dense vertical buildings by the metro waterfront; use the low, open horizon to distinguish the island route. |
| [Official Trailer 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QdBZY2fkU-0) and [Trailer 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VQRLujxTm3c) | Promotional footage shows contrasting beaches, marinas, bridges, street traffic and nightlife; these are edited views. | Use road markings, window grids and traffic frequency as place signals, not just palette changes. |
| [Everglades National Park: Nature](https://www.nps.gov/ever/learn/nature/index.htm) | Freshwater sloughs, pinelands, coastal lowlands and mangroves are distinct ecosystems. | Put an original watershed inland from the roads; reserve its hardest-to-reach section for boardwalk navigation. |

**Uncertain:** Official imagery does not establish a complete road network, relative distances or exact region shapes. No fan-map placements were used.

**Original decisions:** Palmetto State is organized around a brackish inlet, an island causeway and an eastern freight spur. Its landmarks are locally invented. The first release compresses the drivable strip but keeps the regions in physical continuity.
