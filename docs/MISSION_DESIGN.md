# Mission design

| Job | Contact | Win condition | Failure / recovery | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First Light | Mara, Dispatch | Borrow and drive a car; bring the reel through Tidelane | Incapacitation or disabled ride; restart at latest checkpoint or contact | $300 |
| Tail Lights | Mara, Tidelane | Take a Sidecar ride or the loaner Sable, lose pursuit and check in at Bluehour | 58 s, disabled ride or incapacitation | $420 |
| Dead Air | Mara, Bluehour | Climb the west roofs to take the cartridge. Stay on the roof to lose the search or flee, then return | Disabled ride or incapacitation | $570 |
| Cut Across | Roux, causeway | Beat Roux in a lane race using a Sidecar ride fast enough to compete or the loaner Sable | Rival reaches finish, timeout, disabled ride or incapacitation | $630 |
| Last Transmission | Inez, Freight Office | Clear three sentries, platform onto the orange beacon crate, return to Inez | Disabled ride or incapacitation; sentries reset on retry | $850 |

A job's dialogue appears on acceptance; the objective panel and marker persist. The contact becomes available after an ordinary failure or when continuing from the job's starting checkpoint. A cleared screen toast, immediate payout and fresh waypoint confirm completion. Shops and refuges exist physically on the strip rather than in a route picker.
